LadyBugLoop

From a short description, by voice or text, to a complete bug ticket in Jira or GitHub.

Video, 1.5 minutes

  1. The tester describes it, by voice or text. Screen, errors and a recording of the last 60 seconds are captured automatically; the tester can add a reproduction recording.
  2. An agent holds a follow-up conversation with the tester, by voice or text, when a detail is missing.
  3. A ticket ready to fix opens, with everything the developer needs.
10seconds median, from report to a ready ticket. Measured on 30 real reports.

And for the handling: bugloop.studio sends the ticket to a fix (queue, retries, and a conversation with the tester when a fix fails) and back to the tester for approval. In LadyBugLoop itself you can add, as an optional paid add-on, one fix attempt.
